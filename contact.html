Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers to common questions
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. For Kanpur pincodes (208xxx, 209xxx), we offer same-day delivery.
What is the return policy?
7-day easy returns on all products. Raise a return request from your Orders page and we'll process it within 24 hours.
How can I track my order?
Go to My Orders → Select your order → You'll see the complete timeline with tracking ID once dispatched.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept UPI, Credit/Debit cards, Net Banking, Wallets (via Razorpay), and Cash on Delivery.
When will I receive my refund?
Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after return approval. It may take 5-7 days to reflect in your account.
How do I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled before dispatch. Go to My Orders → Select order → Cancel. Raise a ticket if you need help.

5. Returns & Refunds
7-day easy returns policy

We offer a 7-day return policy from the date of delivery on eligible items.

To be eligible for return:

  • Product must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging
  • All tags, labels, and accessories must be intact
  • Return must be requested within 7 days of delivery
  • Proof of purchase (order ID) required

Non-returnable items:

  • Innerwear, lingerie, and swimwear
  • Cosmetics and personal care products (once opened)
  • Customized or personalized products
  • Perishable goods
💡 Refund Time: Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the return is approved and picked up. Amount is credited to original payment method (for COD, refunds are via bank transfer).

Return Process:

  1. Go to My Orders and find the delivered order
  2. Tap "Return" and select a reason
  3. Submit the request — it goes to our admin for approval
  4. Once approved, a pickup will be arranged
  5. After quality check, refund is initiated

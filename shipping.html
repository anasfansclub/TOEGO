❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order?
You can track your order from the "My Orders" section. Once dispatched, you'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email. You can also contact our support for real-time updates.
What if my order is delayed?
We aim for on-time delivery. If there's an unexpected delay due to weather, courier issues, or regional restrictions, we'll notify you via SMS and email. You can also track live status anytime.
Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
Address changes are possible only before the order is dispatched. Please contact support immediately with your order ID if you need to update the address.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes, COD is available for orders up to ₹10,000 in most serviceable pincodes. A small COD handling fee may apply based on the order value.
What if my package arrives damaged?
Please refuse the delivery if packaging is visibly damaged. Contact support immediately with photos — we'll arrange a free replacement or full refund within 48 hours.
